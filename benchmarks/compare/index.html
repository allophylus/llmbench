What the numbers say
  • 27B wins knowledge and precision: GPQA, LiveBench, shell, TermBench (perfect 8/8).
  • A3B wins agentic work and long context: tau2 85 vs 55, and it holds 60 at 110k where the 27B collapses to 36 — a 24-point cliff.
  • Speed: 27B measured live (688 t/s prefill, 34.8 t/s decode). A3B not measured yet — it needs a model swap, so it is shown as —, not estimated.
  • Game: the 27B produced a working three.js game in one shot (79 s, 5,130 tokens, 0 retries) — but the robot scores 0 because bullets and enemies sit on different vertical planes, making hits geometrically impossible. Renders ≠ playable.
