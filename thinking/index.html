thinking on vs off

Does switching the model's reasoning channel on change its answers, and what does it cost? Same server, same temperature 0, same seed, identical token cap; the only variable is the thinking switch. Times are wall-clock seconds; tokens are generated (completion) tokens.

Findings

  • At the 16,384-token cap, thinking lost 4 of 25 questions (22 → 18 correct) while spending 3.3× the tokens.
  • At the 4,096-token cap, thinking lost 2 of 25 questions (19 → 17 correct) while spending 1.6× the tokens.
  • 12 of 25 thinking runs hit the 16,384-token cap without committing; they are scored as wrong, not rescued.
  • One model, one run per arm: read the size of the drop as indicative, the direction as the finding.
