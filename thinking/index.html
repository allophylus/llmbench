thinking on vs off

Both sections ask the same question: does switching the model's reasoning channel on change its answers, and what does it cost? Same server, same temperature 0, same seed, identical token cap; the only variable is the thinking switch. Times are wall-clock seconds; tokens are generated (completion) tokens.

Part 2 — logic traps (everyday questions, 10)

Short questions with an obvious-looking wrong answer: the reasoning test.

Ten one-line questions people actually ask, each with an obvious-looking wrong answer. Every model answers all ten twice: once with its reasoning channel disabled, once enabled. The only difference between the arms is the thinking switch — same server, temperature 0, fixed seed, same token cap (8192), one question per request, fresh context, no examples. Grading is strict: the model's final Answer: line must match the expected answer. No partial credit. A model that runs out of tokens before committing is marked T and left out of the score rather than counted wrong.

Read this before trusting the ranking. Questions 3, 4 and 5 (bat+ball, widgets, lily pads) are the classic Cognitive Reflection Test and are almost certainly in training data; the other seven are fresh everyday traps of the same shape. A model failing a classic item is more damning than failing a fresh one, because it had every chance to memorise it. Passing a classic proves much less. The per-trap matrix marks memorisable items with * so you can judge each result yourself.

3A bat and a ball cost $1.10 together. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?classic CRT item 1 — subtraction instead of algebra5 cents, 0.05, $0.05 …classic CRT *
4If five machines make five widgets in five minutes, how long would it take one hundred machines to make one hundred widgets?classic CRT item 2 — proportional scaling instead of rate5 minutes, five minutes, 5 min …classic CRT *
5A patch of lily pads doubles in size every day and covers the whole lake on day 30. On what day was the lake half covered?classic CRT item 3 — forward doubling instead of inverseday 29, 29, 29th day …classic CRT *
6A doctor gives you three pills and says to take one every 20 minutes. How long until you have taken all three?fencepost / interval count40 minutes, 40 min, 40 mins …fresh
7How many months of the year have 28 days?pragmatic reading — 'exactly 28' vs 'at least 28'all 12, all twelve, 12 …fresh
8A rope ladder hangs off the side of a boat with its rungs 30 cm apart, the bottom rung just touching the water. The tide rises one metre. How many rungs are underwater?tide/boat frame of referencenone, zero, 0 …fresh
